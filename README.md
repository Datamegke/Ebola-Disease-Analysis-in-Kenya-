# Ebola Disease Analysis in Kenya
## Project Description
This project analyses the Ebola Disease in Kenya in the various counties, outlining the population against the 
suspected cases, probable cases, confirmed cases, death cases and the recovered cases. It also displays the percentage 
confirmed cases per 100000 population
### Project Objectives
- Determine the total number of Ebola cases reported across the counties
- Establish the correlation between the population of a place and the spread of Ebola
- Determine the likelihood of recovery/death following an Ebola infection
- Establish the spread rate of Ebola for ever 100000 population of people per county
  ## Dataset Used
  This analysis has been made from data sourced from the Ministry of Health records for the foll0wing counties in Kenya
  1. Nairobi
  2. Mombasa
  3. Kilifi
  4. Machakos
  5. Kakamega
  6. Uasin Gishu
  7. Busia
  8. Siaya
  9. Kisumu
  10. Nakuru
      ## Findings
  - - The total number of Ebola confirmed cases across the country is 72
  - - There is correlation between the population of a given place and the spread of Ebola.
  - - The total number of death cases registerd is 22 while that of recovered cases is 50 against the toll of 72 confirmed cases of Ebola across the country
  - thus the rate of recovery is higher than the rate of death from Ebola.
  - - The county with the highest spread rate of Ebola for every 100000 population of people is Mombasa
